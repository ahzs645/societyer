# Invoice branding assets

The Inter, Inter Display, and Space Mono fonts were obtained from the [ddcnorth brand kit](https://projects.ahmadjalil.com/brandkit/?brand=ddcnorth) on 2026-10-22. Their SIL Open Font License notices are retained here.

The curated logo geometry and palette in `shared/brandKits/ddcnorth.json` come from that kit's published `brand.json`, version `2.3-data-design-connection`. Logo paths are retained as vector geometry. The importer flattens their transforms so the HTML and Forme renderers preserve the same artwork. Importing branding does not change workspace identity or legal billing details.
