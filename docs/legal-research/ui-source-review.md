### BC society register corrections

Under **s. 111(1)** an auditor is compulsory where the bylaws or regulations require one; otherwise appointment is optional. **Section 111(2)** permits the first appointment by directors or ordinary resolution. Subsequent appointments use ordinary resolution at the AGM under **s. 111(3)**. Qualification and independence are separate under **ss. 112–113**. The app now explains the conditional requirement and distinguishes an audit from review and compilation engagements. A newly opened form does not attest independence on the user's behalf. Source ID: `BC-SOC-AUDITOR`. **Confidence: settled core; bylaws, actual engagement and professional authorization need evidence.**

Director conflicts under **s. 56** and senior-manager conflicts under **s. 62** have distinct rules. Section **62(3.1)** permits a requested information-giving role during discussion; it does not give a right to influence the decision or remain for the vote. The disclosure register currently links to directors, so the screen expressly calls for separate officer/senior-manager records where needed. New disclosure forms leave abstention and departure unconfirmed until the user records what occurred. Source IDs: `BC-SOC-CONFLICTS`, `BC-SOC-SENIOR-MANAGER-CONFLICTS`. **Confidence: settled core; transaction facts and exceptions require review.**

The dedicated **s. 197(1)** source describes the member-funded minimum of one director and absence of the ordinary BC residence requirement; **s. 197(2)** disapplies the employee-director restriction. Filed bylaws may require a larger board. The director register and zero-director dashboard flag now select the member-funded source when that status is recorded. Source ID: `BC-SOC-MEMBER-FUNDED-DIRECTORS`. **Confidence: settled core; member-funded status must be legally established.**

### Corporate qualifications

**BC BCA ss. 124–125** require individual adult directors, with the stated incapacity, bankruptcy and specified-conviction rules and exceptions. Disqualification requires prompt resignation. Articles may require share ownership. **CBCA ss. 105(1)–(2), 108(1)(c)** use their own incapacity/bankruptcy rules and cessation mechanism. **OBCA s. 118** has specified legal incapacity findings, bankruptcy and individual/adult requirements; its old residency quota is repealed. These are individual eligibility rules, distinct from minimum board size or the federal resident-Canadian board quota.

The attestation screen now gives the entity's qualification sources and describes annual attestations as optional governance practice unless separately adopted as a requirement. The stored `isAtLeast18` field retains its literal meaning; a possible BC society youth exception is documented separately rather than converting a false age statement into a true one. Unsupported entities receive an explicit governing-Act review state. Source IDs: `BC-BCA-DIRECTOR-QUALIFICATIONS`, `CBCA-DIRECTOR-QUALIFICATIONS`, `OBCA-DIRECTOR-QUALIFICATIONS`. **Confidence: settled core; particular incapacity findings, disqualifications and governing documents require factual review.**
