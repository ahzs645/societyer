# Payroll rules, declarations and operational follow-up

Open **Payroll → Calculator**. The workspace now registers its views through `src/features/payrollCalculation/payrollViewRegistry.ts`. **Customize payroll views** controls their order, visibility, favorites and expanded sections in this browser for the current user and society. Calculation remains available, and a hidden view still opens from its direct link. Preferences contain view identifiers only, not employee or tax data. Adding a trusted module means registering its renderer, permissions and data dependencies; stored preferences cannot run code or introduce a tax formula.

## Maintain a reviewed rule edition

Open **Rules library**, clone the applicable official edition and edit a draft. The structured editor exposes percentages, contribution limits, credits, brackets, effective dates and source evidence. It also supports a validated JSON import/export and comparison. Percentages must use the units shown in the editor. Add the official source URL, review date and evidence before reviewing and publishing. Reviewed and published content is immutable; clone a revision for a correction. An administrator publishes or retires an edition.

In **Calculate**, select the published edition explicitly. The server checks its society, status, payment date, province and CRA method, applies its parameters and retains the complete edition and source evidence with the calculation. Changing relevant sources or retiring the selected edition blocks a stale approval. Leaving the selection on the official edition uses the bundled reviewed CRA rules. The library supports manual maintenance of parameters for the existing ordinary B.C. 2026 engine; a new year, province or employment policy needs a reviewed implementation of its formula too.

Use [CRA T4127 formulas](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/payroll/t4127-payroll-deductions-formulas.html), the applicable CRA contribution limits and the official B.C. employment standards sources. The complete [official source catalogue](payroll-operations-sources.md) records the sources reviewed on October 23, 2026. A society's WorkSafeBC classification, approved reduced EI multiplier, pension/benefit contracts and EHT circumstances require its own actual evidence.

## Import and review TD1 declarations

Open **TD1 import**, choose the employee and federal TD1 or B.C. TD1BC. Read a completed fillable PDF, flattened PDF or clear image locally, or record a manual review of the original. The bundled worker can use local OCR for scans. Select the form year and review every proposed claim and checkbox; an unread checkbox is unresolved, and the parser never verifies a signature. Confirm the signed and dated employee declaration and residence treatment explicitly. Original bytes, identity fields, signatures and raw extracted text are not stored by this importer. Keep the actual signed form in your secure employee records.

The federal total is line 13; B.C. uses line 11. The two concurrent-employer declarations and below-total-claim declarations are independent. Additional requested income tax is a federal amount per payment. A reviewed blank prescribed-zone deduction clears an older value. A T1213 application does not provide CRA authority to reduce withholding.

Claims are amounts used in CRA's calculation, not a flat withholding percentage. CPP, CPP2 and EI are separate from income tax. The screen provides an example of the withholding effect. Applying reviewed settings creates immutable, dated declarations, and payroll resolves the applicable federal and provincial declarations by **payment date**. Later changes create new declarations rather than changing prior payroll snapshots. Review an older valid form's indexed claims explicitly before carrying them into a later withholding year. CRA does not require a new form every year when circumstances are unchanged; relevant changes generally require an updated declaration within seven days.

## Generate and issue an ROE

Open **ROE workflow** and record the actual interruption facts, payroll frequency, last day paid and awareness date. Review the calculated requirement and deadline against the official Service Canada guide, complete the readiness checklist and retain any exceptional treatment. Required cases create or link the existing ROE register obligation and deadline.

Continue to the existing filing tool with the case's facts to generate the validated ROE XML. The case retains its digest and generation details. A generated or downloaded file remains an outstanding issuance obligation. Submit through the society's authorized ROE Web account, or complete the applicable paper process, then record the actual submission and issued serial/acknowledgment. Follow-ups cover acceptance, employee access, missing information, amendments and secure record retention. An amendment creates a linked case and retains the original issued record. General deadline views show these derived ROE obligations as read-only and link to the protected workflow; completing an unrelated dashboard action cannot mark an ROE issued. The app does not determine EI eligibility; employees should apply promptly even if an ROE is outstanding.

## Prepare and reconcile CRA remittances

Open **Remittances** and review the actual outstanding CPP, CPP2, EI and income-tax obligation and CRA remitter schedule. EHT and WorkSafeBC amounts stay separate. Create a payment plan with the society's correct CRA payroll RP account, period, amount, source bank and reviewed liability/expense mappings. Approve the frozen plan. Exporting a preparation record or recording an intended submission does not mark it paid.

After payment actually occurs, match the exact posted bank outflow or record actual CRA payment confirmation, choose the actual settlement date and retain evidence. Partial settlements reduce the remaining obligation, and a new preparation export shows the remaining unpaid amount. An audited cancellation releases only the unpaid commitment if corrected payroll makes the old plan stale; actual settlements stay retained. Duplicate confirmations and bank links are rejected. Optional ledger posting creates balanced entries and retains the bank payment facts against later edits or feed refreshes. Paid obligations retain their original CRA RP account even if the society changes its current settings. Before changing the account, explicitly confirm the actual historical RP account and evidence for any older paid records that lack attribution; the app does not infer their destination from today’s settings.

Bank reconciliation alone is evidence of the outflow; follow up on CRA allocation and confirmation through the relevant account/channel.

The readiness screen records the requirements for future automation: an authorized CRA payroll account and remitter category, correct payroll payee/period, bank or payment-provider agreement, mandates and approval authority, a securely stored credential reference, lead time, idempotency, callbacks, rejection/retry handling and reconciliation. CRA pre-authorized debit is set up through the authorized CRA account; bank-sponsored EDI requires the financial institution's onboarding and CRA test-file qualification. This implementation prepares, approves and reconciles payment records. It does not initiate a bank debit or submit a live CRA payment: that requires an installed, qualified connector and the society's actual mandate and credentials.
