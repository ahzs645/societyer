# Payroll operations: official sources and implementation boundaries

Reviewed and retrieved **2026-10-23**. These sources describe employee declarations, ROE operations and remittance operations. The native deduction formula/rate archive remains `shared/payrollRates/`, with reviewed ordinary British Columbia 2026 coverage. A manually maintained parameter edition cannot extend the formula engine to another province, employment policy, formula era or year.

## TD1 declarations and document review

| Official source | Verified requirements and use |
| --- | --- |
| [2026 federal TD1 PDF](https://www.canada.ca/content/dam/cra-arc/formspubs/pbg/td1/td1-26e.pdf) | Total claim is **line 13**, summing lines 1–12. Basic personal maximum is $16,452; the form directs higher-income individuals to calculate a partial amount. Page 2 contains concurrent-employer and total-income-below-total-claim declarations, prescribed-zone deduction, additional tax per payment, nonresident declaration, certification, signature and date. |
| [2026 TD1BC PDF](https://www.canada.ca/content/dam/cra-arc/formspubs/pbg/td1bc/td1bc-26e.pdf) | Provincial total claim is **line 11**, summing lines 1–10. BC basic personal amount is $13,216. The provincial form independently contains concurrent-employer and below-total-claim declarations, certification, signature and date. Additional withholding is requested on the **federal TD1**. |
| [CRA: Filing Form TD1](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/payroll/set-up-new-employee/filing-form-td1.html) | Obtain forms when an employee starts and within **7 days** of a relevant change to personal tax credits. A new form is not required every year without a relevant change. Review both pages, use the total claim, retain with employee records and **do not send a copy to CRA**. Without a completed form, calculate using the applicable estimated basic personal amount. |
| [CRA: T1213](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t1213.html) and TD1 page 2 | Reducing withholding for deductions/credits outside the TD1 can require a **CRA letter of authority**. An uploaded T1213 application is not an approval. The forms identify an exception for RRSP contributions deducted by the employer from salary. |
| [CRA: Record retention](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/keeping-records/where-keep-your-records-long-request-permission-destroy-them-early.html) | Generally retain required records and support for **six years from the end of the last tax year they relate to**, with longer retention in specified circumstances. Records location and availability requirements apply independently of a successful import. |

The concurrent-employer box prevents claiming the same personal credits twice: federal line 13 or provincial line 11 becomes zero, respectively. The below-total-claim boxes affect the corresponding **income-tax** withholding; they do not exempt CPP/EI. Federal additional tax is a separate amount requested from **each payment**, including when base income-tax withholding is exempt. Federal and provincial declarations must not be collapsed into one checkbox. Employee province of employment determines the applicable employee provincial form; an address alone does not establish that province.

The federal nonresident question asks whether **90% or more of world income** will be included in taxable income earned in Canada. A negative declaration directs a zero federal claim. This is not a reason to silently enable native nonresident payroll: that employment policy requires its own supported calculation. Prescribed-zone amounts and partial-credit worksheets likewise require an explicit reviewed amount and supporting policy.

PDF/text extraction supplies **candidates for review**, not a signed declaration or a tax-law determination. Preserve the actual form year, jurisdiction, total claim, declaration states, extra federal withholding, signature/date review and source reference. Missing or ambiguous checkboxes must remain unresolved. The forms also contain SIN, birth date and address; those identity fields must not leak into generic parsing results, logs, URLs or financial calculation snapshots.

CRA permits employer-created electronic TD1s only when identity is authenticated, the electronic form mirrors the approved form, contains certification and submission date, remains readable/available to CRA, and **cannot be altered after submission** except through a newly submitted TD1. Employer review for reasonableness remains required. A payroll administrator importing a PDF does not by itself establish all those electronic-declaration conditions.

The native engine stores independent `federalMultipleEmployers`, `provincialMultipleEmployers`, `federalTaxExempt` and `provincialTaxExempt` booleans, retaining legacy combined flags as a fallback. CRA's small-income bonus rule supplies a **combined 15%** amount without an authoritative federal/provincial split; a bonus with only one jurisdiction exempt requires a separately reviewed calculation rather than an invented allocation.

## ROE triggers, deadlines and follow-up

The primary source is the [Service Canada guide: How to complete the ROE](https://www.canada.ca/en/employment-social-development/programs/ei/ei-list/reports/roe-guide.html). [ROE Web access](https://www.canada.ca/en/employment-social-development/programs/ei/ei-list/ei-roe/access-roe.html) describes employer access through GCKey or a Sign-in Partner. Employer authorization and successful Service Canada submission remain distinct from generating an XML file.

| Situation | Official rule |
| --- | --- |
| Ordinary seven-day interruption | An employee has had or is anticipated to have **7 consecutive calendar days with no work and no insurable earnings**. Under this rule, the first day of interruption is the **last day for which paid**, not an automatically added next day. |
| Reduced earnings | Salary falls **below 60%** of regular weekly earnings for the guide's specified sick/quarantine, pregnancy/maternity, parental or family-care circumstances. The interruption starts on the **Sunday of that week**. Exactly 60% is not below 60%. A general reduction in hours is not automatically this trigger. |
| Wage-loss insurance | Commencement of WLI payments triggers an interruption. Insurable versus non-insurable WLI and a later second ROE require the guide's separate treatment. |
| Paper ROE | Issue within **5 calendar days** of the first interruption day or the day the employer becomes aware of the interruption. Retain the awareness evidence; use the later applicable date when awareness occurs later. |
| Electronic weekly, biweekly, semimonthly | Issue within **5 calendar days after the end of the pay period containing the interruption**. |
| Electronic monthly or 13 periods/year | Issue by the **earlier** of period-end +5 calendar days and first interruption day +15 calendar days. The guide does not supply a general electronic late-awareness extension. |
| Part-time/on-call/casual | Not every seven-day gap requires an ROE. Issue for an employee request plus an interruption, removal from the active employment list, a Service Canada request, or 30 days with no work/insurable earnings. |
| Vacation and special schedules | The guide gives exceptions for contracted regular annual business vacation with continued employment and specified vacation pay, self-funded leave, nonstandard work schedules and other cases. Resolve the applicable exception explicitly instead of automatically declaring an interruption from a calendar gap. |

Calendar-day deadlines must not silently become business-day deadlines. Unknown interruption facts, unsupported schedules or a Service Canada request without a verified target date require visible review. An amendment's follow-up target must not be presented as a newly invented statutory fixed deadline.

ROE Web supports manual entry and payroll XML upload; Secure Automated Transfer is a separate payroll-service-provider route. Electronic serial prefixes include **W/M** (ROE Web), **S** (SAT) and **Y** (self-employed fishers). Ordinary paper prefixes include legacy **A**, **E** (English), **K** (French) and obsolete **L**; **Z** identifies fishers. A locally generated case/file identifier is not a Service Canada-issued serial.

An issued ROE **cannot be cancelled**. Amend an error, correction or new information, completing **all blocks**. For a paper amendment, block 2 references the original serial. When amending a paper ROE electronically, the guide also requires block 18 to say “Amending a paper ROE” with the original serial. For an ROE submitted in error, use an amendment with “Previous ROE issued in error” in block 18. No new information means no amendment is required.

For paper forms, give part 1 to the employee, send part 2 to Service Canada and retain part 3. For electronic ROEs, Service Canada already receives the record; the employee can view/print it in **My Service Canada Account** and should not submit a duplicate paper copy. Tell employees to apply for EI promptly after an interruption even if all ROEs have not arrived. An ROE does not itself determine EI benefit eligibility. Retain related payroll records/electronic data and paper part 3 securely for **6 years after the year to which they relate**.

## Remittance scheduling, payment and confirmation

| Official source | Verified requirements and limits |
| --- | --- |
| [CRA: When to remit](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/payroll/remitting-source-deductions/how-when-remit-due-dates.html) | Use the employer's assigned remitter type. Regular monthly remittance is due on the next month's 15th; threshold 1 is the 25th for days 1–15 and the next month's 10th for days 16–end; threshold 2 is the third working day after each of days 7, 14, 21 and month-end. CRA-recognized holiday/weekend due dates roll to the next business day. Quarterly eligibility is a separate confirmed condition. |
| [CRA: How to remit](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/payroll/remitting-source-deductions/how-when-remit-make-payment.html) | Threshold 2 must remit electronically or at a Canadian financial institution. Current source deductions and assessed arrears use **separate payment allocations**. Nil remittance is its own report, not a zero-dollar payment. A printed app voucher is not an original micro-encoded bank remittance voucher. |
| [CRA: Scheduled PAD](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/about-canada-revenue-agency-cra/pay-authorized-debit.html) | Payroll source deductions are eligible. The first withdrawal must be at least **5 business days** after agreement creation. Modify, cancel or skip at least **5 business days** before the withdrawal. A business owner uses My Business Account; **business representatives cannot schedule PADs for businesses they represent**. The agreement requires authority over the bank account. |
| [CRA: Bank/credit-union online payment](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/about-canada-revenue-agency-cra/pay-online-banking.html) | Use the bank's supported business payroll payee/remitter type and **15-character business RP account**. Payments may count the same or next business day depending on the bank; payroll's due-date page defines Internet-bank receipt as the day the institution **credits CRA**. Bank scheduling is separate from CRA PAD. |
| [CRA: Third-party services](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/about-canada-revenue-agency-cra/pay-credit-card.html) | Provider support varies by payment type and fees/lead times vary. The employer remains responsible for CRA receipt by the due date. CRA does not endorse listed companies. A provider request/acceptance does not prove CRA received the payment. |
| [CRA: EDI provider onboarding](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/about-canada-revenue-agency-cra/third-party-service-providers-cra.html) | A prospective remittance provider must arrange sponsorship/documentation through a listed bank trading partner, coordinate a test file for each remittance type, and obtain successful CRA testing confirmation before production EDI transmissions. Public website listing has additional history/volume criteria and is a separate step. |
| [CRA: Confirm a payment](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/payments/payments-cra/confirm-payment.html) | Allow processing time and verify the actual RP account/balance in the CRA business account. Retain confirmation/reference evidence. A mismatch can require transfer/correction; local bank withdrawal and CRA account posting are different events. |

CRA's PAD page says a payment is considered paid on the withdrawal day, with payments after **18:00 Eastern**, weekends or statutory holidays processed on the next business day. It states CRA account updates can take up to **2 business days** for PAD; the bank page allows up to **3 business days** for bank payments. Third-party deemed-paid timing depends on the provider. Scheduling must account for both the statutory deadline and the specific payment method's lead time, Canadian holidays and time zone.

The application retains the actual RP account, review evidence, confirming actor and time on a paid obligation. Its remaining balance continues to use that **original account** after global settings change; a new unpaid period uses the current account. An older paid record with no retained RP account requires an explicit `confirmLegacyAccount` review of the original CRA statement/payment receipt. That review preserves the existing amount/date and cannot reassign an already confirmed account. Unknown legacy attribution blocks payment edits, clearing and a global RP-account change. A later real CRA transfer requires its own reviewed correction rather than rewriting the historical destination.

Prepared instructions distinguish the original approved commitment, actual payments already recorded and the **remaining amount** to submit. A partial payment never authorizes exporting the full original commitment as another debit. A CRA-confirmation-only settlement can record receipt evidence but cannot create a bank journal without an exactly matched actual bank outflow. The bank transaction date alone does not establish the legal CRA receipt date.

An approved, exported or manually submitted plan also blocks the legacy payment editor and clearing route, including before the first settlement. Payment changes must use the reviewed settlement flow. Export independently rechecks the unpaid commitment against the live outstanding balance and other commitments, so older inconsistent records cannot authorize an excessive instruction.

These official sources do **not** describe a universal anonymous CRA payroll-payment API. The PAD EFILE web service described on the PAD page is a signed **T185 current-year individual** arrangement, excluding instalments/T2; it does not authorize repurposing that API for employer payroll remittances. Automatic execution requires a contracted and qualified bank/provider route, actual employer/account authority, supported remittance payloads, credentials, testing and payment reconciliation. Preparation, scheduled, provider accepted, withdrawn and CRA-confirmed states must remain distinguishable. No research or development qualification authorizes a live debit, filing or external employee message.
