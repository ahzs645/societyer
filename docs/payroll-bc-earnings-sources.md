# B.C. earnings and employer cost rules

The native implementation in `shared/payrollEarnings.ts` is original code based on the official sources below, reviewed October 23, 2026. The supplied payroll proposal was treated as reference material. No external payroll engine or AGPL implementation was copied.

## Scope and records

`calculateBcEarnings` supports ordinary B.C. employment, work from June 2024 through December 2026, and explicit integer minutes and CAD cents. Payment-year deductions are a separate engine: December work paid in January retains December earnings rules. Averaging agreements, overtime exemptions, specialized industries, substituted holidays and collective agreement substitutions require a separately reviewed engine and produce blockers.

The complete work context must cover every Sunday–Saturday week touching the selected pay period. Zero-hour holiday records are required because a day off can still earn statutory holiday pay. A complete preceding 30-day wage-history attestation is required for holiday/sick eligibility and averages. Missing records are never replaced with an invented average. Daily totals cannot establish shift rest; the user must review eight hours between shifts and 32 consecutive weekly hours free from work. `restPremiumMinutes` is explicitly the remaining ordinary time requiring a weekly-rest premium, excluding time already paid at daily, weekly or holiday premium rates. An overlapping total is rejected.

## Rounding and full cost

Work amounts retain exact integer ratios and round the period work total once to cents; the largest-remainder method assigns the resulting cents to detailed work lines, with source order breaking ties. Vacation rounds once for each percentage cohort and distributes paid vacation by largest remainders across earnings dates, preserving the four/six-percent anniversary split. Salary dates retain their covered-work and paid-leave allocation.

Final vacation pays both the prior accrued balance and current earned vacation, clears the new unpaid accrual, and releases the prior liability without recognizing its expense twice. Full employer cost equals cash remuneration less previously expensed vacation paid, additional unpaid vacation accrual, employerCPP/CPP2/EI, actual WorkSafe/EHT and additional actual benefits/pension costs. Employee withholding is absent from employer expense additions. Benefits/pension already included in cash remuneration are explicitly deducted from additional costs; noncash benefits still require actual employer cost. EHT period accrual is the difference between cumulative liability before and after the current B.C. remuneration and requires an annual employer/location reconciliation. Missing actual employer configuration produces blockers.

`allocatePayrollEmployerCosts` requires 100% explicit allocations and conserves every component and total cent. It uses largest remainders per component, preserving source order for equal remainders. The backend validates owned fund/grant references before storing/posting; the standalone Tools preview accepts human reference text for supporting exports.

## Focused evidence

Run `npx tsx scripts/check-payroll-earnings.ts`. It checks full-week interaction, partial pay periods, holiday/rest non-duplication, salary funding and rational rates, salary leave credits, partial-day sick leave, five-year reconciliation and cent rounding, minimum-wage dates, final-pay timestamps, EHT boundaries and qualification, WorkSafe caps, complete cost allocations, mileage tier crossings, classification and financial-period disclosure.


The [CRA continuous-vacation guidance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/payroll/payroll-deductions-contributions/special-payments/vacation-pay-public-holidays.html) requires the bonus/irregular method for CPP and income tax on vacation paid continuously or without vacation leave. Generated per-cheque vacation, anniversary cash adjustments and final vacation payouts therefore carry `deductionTreatment: bonus`; EI remains subject to its ordinary annual cap. Additional actual vacation-leave earnings can retain regular treatment. The official source is archived by the deductions owner in `shared/payrollRates/vacation-pay.html`.

Supplementary bonus runs use server-selected `adjustments_only` mode: no ordinary work, automatic holiday, sick leave, salary or anniversary replay. They calculate only the reviewed additional earnings and the vacation earned on those amounts.


Fixed-salary calendar bounds are retained separately from the actual earnings end. Partial employment uses calendar-day proration only when the contract basis, confirmation and evidence are supplied. The normal earning-period start is explicit; it is not inferred backwards from a payment date that may include a lag. A final calculation can pay before the normal calendar end when employment has already ended. If the resulting allocated salary is insufficient for the work-date minimum wage, or for the actual straight-time wage underlying covered overtime premiums, an explicit wage-floor earning adds the shortfall and also earns vacation.


Reviewed paid annual leave can be recorded in minutes already included in fixed salary, linked to an approved/taken leave request or explicit reviewed evidence. These minutes do not count as worked hours for daily or weekly overtime and do not create extra cash or a second leave-time deduction. The monetary portion already included in salary releases the available, source-reconciled prior vacation liability once; any new vacation earned remains a separate current-period expense. A full salary-paid vacation week is supported without inventing worked hours.

Ordinary accrued-vacation cash is an explicit reviewed payout relation with an available prior balance, source evidence and actual leave/cashout treatment. Actual vacation leave follows regular deduction treatment; unused vacation paid without leave follows CRA bonus treatment. The existing liability is released rather than expensed again, and a simultaneous final payout pays only its residual balance. Arbitrary additional earning codes labelled vacation cannot bypass that relation.

The employer setup persists complete qualifying nonprofit physical locations, their remuneration openings and eligibility/source evidence. Employee remuneration uses the reviewed primary annual reporting location or documented reasonable attribution. Native previews retain before/current/after remuneration and tax by location; finalized and reversed runs preserve signed location bases. The annual view waits for every recorded-payroll page and explicitly rejects ambiguous legacy attribution unless represented in a reviewed opening. Run `npx tsx scripts/check-payroll-eht-locations.ts` for native integration on both portable database runtimes, and `npx playwright test -c playwright.payroll-eht.config.ts` for saved settings and visible exact native reconciliation.


`deriveBcWageHistory` in `shared/payrollWageHistory.ts` derives daily average-day-pay wages from the retained native earnings input and actual emitted amounts. It excludes the full ordinary overtime wage, including straight time embedded in salary, using the frozen exact rate and covered premium minutes rather than the rounded hourly display. Exact salary-overtime amounts round once as a cohort and distribute cents by largest remainders. Paid ordinary salary leave and statutory holiday wages remain included. The Act’s definition of “overtime wages” points to sections 37 and 40; holiday work under section 46 is retained, including its holiday premium. Unknown custom cash classifications, lump-sum vacation without a daily earned/taken allocation, missing amounts and blocked calculations require reviewed daily history rather than invented zero wages. The server combines frozen prior calculations and chronological current-period actual earnings before accepting a holiday/sick average. Run `npx tsx scripts/check-payroll-wage-history.ts` for the cross-run fifteen-day holiday and full salary-overtime regressions. Rules edition: `bc-ordinary-2026.5`.
